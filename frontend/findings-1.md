One caution, though: **the 5% Treasury Bill mechanism needs to be validated with Nigerian securities and cooperative regulations before you put it in front of investors as a finalized operating model**. The exact structure—who legally owns the T-Bills, who executes the investment, whether NEXUS is an investment manager or technology provider, how returns are distributed, and what licenses/partnerships are required—will materially affect the feasibility. I would present it initially as a **proposed regulated partnership model**, not as if NEXUS itself will directly pool and manage member funds.

The next thing I would do is turn this into a **complete NEXUS business model**: the actual user journey for a cooperative member, how a cooperative joins the network, how the 5% investment mechanism works, how the Financial Passport is built, **what NEXUS charges**, how NEXUS makes money without exploiting cooperatives, and then identify **2–3 truly novel features** that could become your "wow factor" for the investor pitch.
